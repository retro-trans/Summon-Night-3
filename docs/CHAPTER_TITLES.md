# Chapter-title translation

All 30 distinct title cards were translated and independently reviewed. Their 31 native instances are in `02.DAT`, packs 92–122; pack 92 is an alternate copy of Chapter 2. Each pack contains one 480×272 indexed texture and one audio resource.

The built-in image generator redrew each complete card using the native artwork as its reference. Import performs full-canvas downsampling and original-palette quantization; it does not add painted rectangles or replacement text overlays. All 31 audio resources and decoded pack sizes are unchanged.

The original English subtitles are preserved, including their unusual phrasing and capitalization. No new character, location, or organization spellings were introduced by these title translations. The meaning review documents number-neutral Japanese and the wider nuance of “Coward.”

## Scope

This covers the complete contiguous native title-card family, including 16 numbered chapters, the final chapter, five side stories, one bonus title and seven ending titles. The duplicate Chapter 2 instance is also replaced. The executable separately contains 23 heading/title pairs; their save/gallery consumers and available layout widths remain unverified and those metadata strings are not changed in this artwork build. No claim is made that every possible title consumer or the rest of the game is translated.
